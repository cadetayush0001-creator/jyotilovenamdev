From My Heart

Dear Naamdev...

Kuch baatein saamne kehna mushkil hota hai, isliye aaj dil khol kar keh rahi hoon.

Naamdev,

Pata nahi main apni feelings ko words mein exactly kaise explain karun, lekin ek baat main dil se kehna chahti hoon — you mean so much to me.

Meri life mein tumhara hona mere liye sirf ek relationship nahi hai. Tum meri khushi ho, meri smile ki wajah ho, aur meri zindagi ka woh beautiful part ho jise main kabhi khona nahi chahti.

Tumhare saath bitaya hua har moment mere liye special hai. Kabhi tumhari ek chhoti si smile, kabhi tumhari care, kabhi tumhari baatein... Pata nahi kab ye sab meri life ka sabse beautiful part ban gaya.

Shayad main har baar express nahi kar paati, lekin mere dil mein tumhare liye bahut saara pyaar hai. ❤️

Thank you for being there.
Thank you for understanding me.
Thank you for making my life more beautiful.

I LOVE YOU, NAAMDEV. ❤️

Always Yours,
Jyoti ❤️
